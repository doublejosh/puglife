A Pug SanctuaryThe Abbey of the Wrinkled Lord

Your chores, as decreed by His Wrinkledness.

You are the sole caretaker of a misty island monastery run by lazy, snorting, deeply revered pugs. Your real tasks arrive as solemn Decrees. Finish them in real life and the sanctuary grows from a cramped cottage into a sprawling, absurd dog-empire.

  • Mobile-first web app
  • Real tasks, not pretend ones
  • Gentle, never punishing
  • Pastel-gothic SVG art
The premise

A serene sanctuary. Underneath, a cult of supreme laziness.

On the surface it looks like a postcard: stone cloisters, rolling hills, cute little dog houses, fog over cobblestones. Underneath, it's an empire of snorting and logistical chaos, run by robed monks who are entirely subordinate to fat little dogs.

The game plays the absurdity completely straight. Pugs are medieval royalty. A monk treats an ear-scratch like a papal bull. Your tax return becomes "The Annual Reckoning of Coin."

How it works

Three layers. Only one of them is about real life.

Real progress opens the door. Play makes going through it fun.

Real tasks

Decrees

Your actual to-dos (taxes, a mural, an oil change), presented as wax-sealed scrolls from the Wrinkled Lord.

Grows buildings, brings new pugs, earns Kibble and Devotion.

In-game quests

Pilgrimages

2–5 minute set pieces that permanently change the island. Each one stays locked until your Devotion reaches its threshold.

Opens new areas, changes the lighting and weather, grants abilities.

The fidget

Tending

Belly rubs, wrinkle wiping, candle lighting. Something pleasant to do with your thumbs next to the task list.

A little Kibble, capped daily. Never any Devotion.

The island

You start in a cramped cottage. Lord Chonk is lying on the bridge.

Permanent misty twilight with drizzle. Tap the ground to walk. Expansion plots, bought with Kibble, extend the edges.

Open at the start Locked until a Pilgrimage One lot per Decree
Pilgrimages

Three set pieces, each unlocked by real-world Devotion.

Play them in any order once they're open. Each one changes the island for good.

Devotion 5

The Great Snore Synchronization

How it plays
Take the antique brass microphone from the belfry and collect snore samples from sleeping pugs, each with its own pitch. Then tune the resonance organ's baffles to keep the waveform under the "glass crack" line.
Unlocks
The Dormitory Catacombs: heated sleeping crypts and rare nocturnal pugs.
Devotion 12

The Sacred Treat Tribute

How it plays
Forage a gull-guarded parsnip, cliff honey and crunchy seaweed. At the tribute oven, hold a temperature band and a crunchiness band at once. Get it wrong and Lord Chonk sighs theatrically.
Unlocks
Lord Chonk waddles aside, the Orchard opens, and twilight turns to a golden autumn afternoon.
Devotion 20

The Tail-Curl Calibration

How it plays
The weather vane reads the pugs' tails. Herd stubborn pugs into alignment circles, one tile at a time. Some move only when bribed. One moves only backwards.
Unlocks
The drizzle stops. Hot springs bubble up and grant Warm Paws, Steam Hop and the Pug Cart.

Later slots: The Wrinkle Census (opens the Library of Folds), The Ear Flop Inquisition, The Midnight Zoomies Procession.

  • Best friendsThey follow you in a waddling procession.

1–2 Kibble per action, about 30 a day at most, and never any Devotion. Only real tasks move the world forward.

Roadmap

Eight phases. Each one ships something usable.

The task manager comes first and the game is layered on after. Data stays on the device until the last phase.

  1. 1

    Decree core

    Task list, editor, recurrence, CSV import, IndexedDB.

    Done when your real tasks survive a reload and the copy makes you smile.

  2. 2

    The island shell

    SVG map, camera, tap-to-walk, a lot per Decree with growing buildings.

    Done when every Decree has a building you can walk to.

  3. 3

    Ceremony, pugs, mood

    Completion ceremony, pug arrivals, monks, fog and candles.

    Done when finishing a task feels like a coronation.

  4. 4

    Tending

    Context actions, pug wants, friendship, the daily cap.

    Done when 2–5 minutes of fidgeting feels good.

  5. 5

    Pilgrimages

    Thresholds, the catacombs layer, climate system, all three set pieces.

    Done when each one visibly changes the island for good.

  6. 6

    Shop and decor

    Spend Kibble, place decor on a grid, buy expansion plots.

    Done when the sanctuary feels like your own absurd empire.

  7. 7

    Art and audio

    Final pastel-gothic art, optional sound and haptics, accessibility, PWA.

    Done when it looks and sounds finished.

  8. 8

    Sync

    Magic-link sign-in and a small Supabase backend; local stays the offline cache.

    Done when a task finished on the phone shows up on desktop.
